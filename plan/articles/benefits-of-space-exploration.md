Some examples of how space benefits Canadians and all of humanity.

![A man holding the Earth in his hands](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/images/a-propos/retombees-de-l-espace-au-quotidien/defaut-main-terre-id-16729-sm.jpg)  

### [Improving health care](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/improving-health-care.asp)

Experiments performed in space help us understand health problems on Earth.

### [Protecting our planet and our environment](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/protecting-our-planet-and-our-environment.asp)

Satellites provide data on climate change, measure pollution, and help protect our planet.

### [Creating scientific and technical jobs](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/creating-scientific-and-technical-jobs.asp)

The space sector generates high-tech jobs for Canadians.

### [Improving our day-to-day lives](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/improving-our-day-to-day-lives.asp)

Space technologies improve products we use every day, weather forecasts, and communications worldwide.

### [Enhancing safety on Earth](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/enhancing-safety-on-earth.asp)

Satellites data can be used to predict natural disasters and to support emergency relief efforts.

### [Making scientific discoveries](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/making-scientific-discoveries.asp)

Scientific breakthroughs are challenging our assumptions and pushing our boundaries by exploring the unknown.

### [Sparking youth's interest in science](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/sparking-youth-interest-in-science.asp)

Astronauts encourage young people to study science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

### [Cooperating with countries around the world](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/about/everyday-benefits-of-space-exploration/cooperating-with-countries-around-the-world.asp)

Canada is a partner of the International Space Station, a research laboratory in space.
