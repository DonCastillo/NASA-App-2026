## Description

This study will assess injury risk during landing by surveying crew members about any [discomfort they may experience during re-entry](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasa-to-test-telemedicine-gather-essential-health-data-with-polaris-dawn-crew/) to Earth and combining that information with sensor data from the spacecraft. This work specifically focuses on landing because it’s the most dynamic event of flight missions.

## Impact

This information will influence future simulations and models to improve predictive injury work and help NASA design re-entry protocols and standards to protect crews as missions venture to the Moon, Mars, and beyond.

**Type of study:** Flight

*Formal project title:* *Spacecraft Occupant Risk*
